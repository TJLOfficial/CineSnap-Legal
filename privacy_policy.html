Last updated: 11.8.2026

The short version: CineSnap collects nothing. The app has no internet permission at all, so it is technically incapable of sending your videos, images, or any other information off your device. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no user accounts.

2. What data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. We have no servers, no database, and no analytics service. We cannot see how you use the app.

3. How the app handles your files

Everything CineSnap does happens locally on your device:

  • Videos you choose are decoded by your phone's own media decoders. The video file is never copied off the device.
  • Frames you save are written to your device's own storage, in Pictures/CineSnap. They are your files; you can move or delete them like any other photo.
  • Prompt notes, favourites, and app settings are stored only in CineSnap's private storage on your device. They are removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Because the app has no network access, none of this can leave your phone.

4. Permissions and why they are needed

Permission Why
Photos and videos (read) To list the videos already on your phone in the Videos tab, and to list every frame you have saved in Pictures/CineSnap in the Library. Read-only: the app never modifies media you did not create with it.
Limited photo/video access (Android 14+) If you prefer, you can grant access to selected items only. The app works with either choice.
Billing To offer the one-time Pro purchase through Google Play.

You can decline the media permissions and still use the app: choosing a video through Android's system photo picker requires no permission at all.

CineSnap does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or device identifiers.

5. No advertising, no analytics, no AI models

CineSnap contains no advertising SDK, no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no third-party trackers. We do not use your Advertising ID, IP address, or location — the app cannot access them in any way that leaves the device.

CineSnap also contains no artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. Features such as sharpness detection, colour-palette extraction, and tone matching are ordinary mathematical image processing that runs on your device. Nothing is sent to an AI service, and no model is downloaded.

6. Purchases through Google Play

The optional one-time Pro purchase is processed entirely by Google Play, not by us. When you buy, Google handles your payment and processes the related data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We never see your name, address, or payment details.

From Google we receive only what Play Console shows every developer: aggregated sales figures and order identifiers used for refunds and support. See the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Data retention

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Files and notes created by the app stay on your device until you delete them.

8. Your rights under the GDPR

As a person in the EU you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. These rights apply to data a company holds about you.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or erase on your behalf. The frames and notes on your device are under your control, not ours. If you have a question or believe we have processed data about you, contact temetunes.dev@gmail.com and we will respond.

For matters relating to your purchase, contact Google, which acts as the controller for that data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto), tietosuoja.fi.

9. Children

CineSnap is a tool for video creators and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone — including children — because we collect no data at all.
